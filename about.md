# About

This is a small public archive for sustained work in mathematics, computation, and learning.
The emphasis is on arguments that can be reconstructed: definitions are stated explicitly,
proofs keep their dependencies visible, and examples are used to expose failure modes.

The site is intentionally selective. It publishes finished conceptual notes, while private
working instructions, review logs, and learning-state records remain outside the public archive.
